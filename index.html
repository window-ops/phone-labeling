Rules and How to Play:

Welcome to the Phone Labeling Game! Your goal is to correctly label randomly generated phones based on the following criteria:

  • If the phone lasts less than 5 years or is not fully repairable, it's planned obsolescence.
  • If the phone is desirable for less than its actual lasting - 2 years, it is perceived obsolescence.
  • Each phone labeled correctly earns you +1 point, while each incorrect label deducts -1 point. Reach 0 points to lose the game.

Please note that the game is not realistic. The vast majority of phones will contain both perceived and planned obsolescence, and companies such as Fairphone are looking to build phones that are not only designed to last, but also are sustainable.
